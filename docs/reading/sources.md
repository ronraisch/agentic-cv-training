It records embedded hyperlink targets as well as visible URLs. Duplicate source URLs share a destination; occurrence-level provenance remains in [source-map.json](source-map.json).

**Migration form:** external articles/PDF topics are covered by original local chapters, not verbatim copied publications. Complete team learner handouts are preserved as local HTML. External links in this table and chapter footers are provenance only. Video/course links and dataset/exercise acquisition links remain external where appropriate. This is a link/topic coverage inventory, not a claim that every external publication was fetched or mirrored.
