# Research training — one starting point


The program has four parts. Each syllabus links to local theory, the relevant course videos,
current exercises and mentor conversations. Active materials are in English.

**The reading is for you; completing every page is not mandatory.** Use it to fill gaps and
explore the theory while working with your approved AI agent. If you can explain the concepts,
reason through a new example and answer the mentor's questions yourself, that is enough.
Theory and video learning remain substantial parts of onboarding; producing working code alone
is not a substitute for understanding.

## Open the reading

Open [the reading home](reading/index.md) directly on GitHub. All stage and chapter links use
rendered Markdown, including local images, tables and equations. No clone or download is needed.
For the offline browser edition, open `docs/reading/index.html` in a local clone. HTML files
are source files on GitHub; their `.md` companions are the readable GitHub edition.

The shelf contains 27 original explanatory chapters and 12 complete historical learner handouts
converted to HTML. New chapters consolidate duplicate explanations and cover the previously
listed subjects, including bias, metrics, classical models, unsupervised learning, generative
models and 3D. Historical handouts retain their full text, with reading links redirected to local
explanations. Their old assignment and scheduling instructions are historical; current required
work is in the stage syllabi and linked labs.

[Source coverage](reading/sources.md) maps the original links to local destinations and records
unresolved internal resources. [The machine-readable inventory](reading/source-map.json) retains
occurrence-level provenance. Original Drive documents are preserved. Videos retain their source
URLs; data/exercise acquisition references remain external where appropriate.

## Work and discuss

Read or watch the relevant explanation before and during an exercise. Ask your agent to derive
an unfamiliar step, compare alternatives and propose a counterexample. Verify it with an actual
calculation, input or experiment, and explain the result yourself. The manual Python capstone
and first SAM2 service retain their existing manual boundaries.

Each part has an initial foundations/application conversation and a later deeper conversation.
The mentor continues using the existing theory question banks, derivations and gotchas. Those
banks stay with the mentor; learner pages are study material, not an answer key.
Record knowledge gaps and revisit just those topics.
